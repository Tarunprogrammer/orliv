# Urban Rooftop Organic Farming: Waterproofing, Drainage & Root Protection

## 1. Terrace Slab Waterproofing Integrity

Waterproofing is the foundational safeguard of any rooftop garden. Continuous water contact, root exudates, and microbial moisture can degrade untreated concrete slabs, causing internal rebar corrosion (spalling) and ceiling dampness below.

### Primary Waterproofing Systems for Rooftops:
1. **Elastomeric Polyurethane (PU) Liquid Membranes**:
   - Seamless, highly flexible coating capable of bridging substrate cracks up to 2mm.
   - Resists UV radiation and standing water.
   - Recommended minimum dry film thickness (DFT): 1.2 mm to 1.5 mm.
2. **APP/SBS Modified Bituminous Membranes**:
   - 3mm to 4mm multi-layer torch-applied bituminous sheets with polyester reinforcement.
   - High puncture resistance, suitable for intensive garden terrace beds.
3. **Food-Grade Epoxy/Cementitious Acrylic Coatings**:
   - Double-coat polymer-modified cementitious slurry applied before laying protective screeds.

## 2. Root Barrier Systems & Protection

Aggressive root systems from urban crops (particularly woody perennial herbs, fig trees, moringa, and taproots) secrete weak organic acids and search for microscopic hairline fissures in concrete.

### Root Barrier Specifications:
- **High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) Root Barrier Sheets**: Minimum thickness of **1.0 mm to 1.5 mm**. Non-toxic, puncture-resistant, and impervious to root penetration.
- **Continuous Seam Welding / Overlapping**: Overlap root barrier joints by at least 150 mm and seal with hot-air welding or butyl-based waterproof adhesive tapes.
- **Turn-Up at Parapet Edges**: Root barriers and waterproofing membranes must extend at least **150 mm (6 inches) above the finished soil line** along perimeter walls and parapets.

## 3. High-Efficiency Drainage & Sub-Surface Water Management

Poor drainage causes saturated stagnant water, promoting root rot (*Phytophthora*, *Pythium*) and adding dangerous sustained dead load to the rooftop.

### Layered Multi-Tier Drainage Assembly:
1. **Polypropylene Drainage Cells (Dimple / Honeycomb Drain Core)**:
   - 20 mm to 30 mm interlocking dimpled HDPE/PP drainage sheets.
   - Compressive strength: > 150–200 kN/m² to bear soil and planter loads.
   - Provides an unobstructed 95% void space for rapid horizontal water movement towards drains.
2. **Non-Woven Needle-Punched Geotextile Filter Fabric**:
   - Weight: **120 to 150 GSM (grams per square meter)**.
   - Function: Placed directly over drainage cells to prevent fine vermicompost and cocopeat particles from washing down, preventing drain pipe clogging while ensuring rapid vertical water percolation.
3. **Elevated Stand Architecture for Container Gardening**:
   - Avoid resting grow bags directly on raw terrace tiles or slabs.
   - Use **Galvanized Iron (GI) or Heavy-Duty Plastic Pot Stands (elevated 5 to 10 cm / 2 to 4 inches)** with rubber foot pads.
   - Benefits:
     * Maintains complete air circulation beneath containers (air-pruning of roots).
     * Eliminates algae, fungal slime, and damp moisture rings on roof tiles.
     * Allows unhindered sweeping and inspection of terrace floor drains.

---

## 4. Slope and Downspout Maintenance

- Maintain a minimum floor slope of **1:50 to 1:100** towards rainwater collection hoppers.
- Install stainless steel or brass dome grates over downspouts with gravel filter collars to stop fallen leaf debris and organic mulch from entering rainwater harvesting lines.
