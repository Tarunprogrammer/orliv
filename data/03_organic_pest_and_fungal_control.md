# Urban Rooftop Organic Farming: Organic Pest and Fungal Control

## 1. Botanical and Natural Insect Control

Urban rooftop gardens are enclosed micro-ecosystems where synthetic chemicals must never be used. Synthetic insecticides destroy predatory ladybugs, predatory wasps, and pollinators, causing rapid secondary pest surges and container soil toxicity.

### 1. Cold-Pressed Pure Neem Oil Foliar Spray (Azadirachtin):
- **Active Mechanism**: Antifeedant, insect growth regulator (IGR), repels aphids, mealybugs, whiteflies, spider mites, and caterpillars.
- **Recipe & Formulation (per 1 Liter Water)**:
  * **5 ml** of pure cold-pressed unrefined Neem Oil (containing 1,500 to 10,000 ppm Azadirachtin).
  * **2 ml** of organic liquid emulsifier (pure Potassium Castile soap or natural Reetha / Soapnut fruit extract).
  * **1 Liter** of lukewarm water (approx. 30–35°C facilitates thorough emulsification).
- **Preparation Method**: Whisk neem oil and emulsifier together until a cloudy milky emulsion forms, then blend thoroughly into the water.
- **Application Guidelines**:
  * Spray once every 7 to 10 days for maintenance, or every 3 to 4 days during active infestation.
  * Always apply during early morning (before 7:30 AM) or late evening (after 5:30 PM) to avoid phytotoxic foliar sunburn and avoid direct contact with foraging honeybees.
  * Spray the undersides of leaves and nodal junctions thoroughly.

### 2. Ginger-Garlic-Chilli (GGC) Bio-Repellent (Agniastra Blend):
- **Target Pests**: Heavy caterpillar infestations, fruit borers, and persistent leaf miners.
- **Recipe**:
  * 50g fresh crushed Garlic, 50g fresh crushed Ginger, and 50g hot Green Chillies (rich in capsaicin).
  * Boil or soak in 500 ml water for 24 hours, strain through fine muslin cloth.
  * Dilute **15–20 ml of concentrate per 1 Liter of water** and add 1 ml liquid soap.

## 2. Organic Bio-Fungicides & Disease Management

Rooftop plants experience intense heat, dew condensation, and sudden humidity shifts, triggering fungal diseases such as powdery mildew (*Erysiphales*), downy mildew, leaf spots (*Cercospora*), and early blight (*Alternaria*).

### 1. Fermented Sour Buttermilk (Khatti Chhachh / Sour Whey):
- **Active Mechanism**: High concentration of lactic acid bacteria (*Lactobacillus*), bacteriocins, and organic lactic acids that suppress fungal spores and activate systemic acquired resistance (SAR).
- **Preparation Protocol**:
  * Place 1 Liter of raw, unpasteurized natural buttermilk / whey in an earthenware pot or copper vessel for **3 to 5 days** in a shady spot until pungent and distinctly sour.
  * (Optional): Submerging a clean copper strip in the buttermilk forms copper lactate, imparting mild fungicidal copper ion properties.
- **Dilution & Application**:
  * Dilute **100 ml of fermented buttermilk with 900 ml of clean water** (1:10 ratio).
  * Strain carefully to avoid clogging sprayer nozzles.
  * Spray weekly on tomato, pepper, squash, and cucumber foliage.
  * Highly effective against powdery mildew, downy mildew, and leaf curl virus vectors.

### 2. Sodium / Potassium Bicarbonate Fungicidal Wash:
- **Recipe**: 4 grams (approx. 1 teaspoon) Potassium Bicarbonate or Baking Soda + 3 ml Horticultural Oil / Neem Oil + 1 Liter Water.
- **Mechanism**: Alters foliar surface pH to alkaline (>8.0), preventing spore germination of powdery mildew and black spot.

## 3. Physical & Biological Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

1. **Chromatic Sticky Traps**:
   - **Yellow Sticky Traps**: Positioned 6 inches above crop canopies to catch winged aphids, whiteflies, fungus gnats, and leaf miners.
   - **Blue Sticky Traps**: Essential for attracting and monitoring thrips (*Frankliniella occidentalis*).
   - Density: 1 trap per 2 to 3 square meters of terrace space.
2. **Fruit Fly Pheromone Traps (Methyl Eugenol & Cuelure)**:
   - For cucurbits (gourds, melons) and solanaceous crops (tomatoes, brinjals), hang pheromone traps 1 meter above floor level to mass-trap male *Bactrocera* fruit flies.
3. **Biological Control Agents**:
   - Soil drench of *Trichoderma harzianum* or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* (5g/liter) to combat root rot and damping-off fungi in seedling trays.
