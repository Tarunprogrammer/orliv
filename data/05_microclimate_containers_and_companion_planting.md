# Urban Rooftop Organic Farming: Containers, Microclimates & Companion Planting

## 1. Container Sizing and Material Selection

Selecting the right container optimizes plant root architecture while preventing excessive rooftop dead weight.

### Container Specifications:
1. **UV-Stabilized Virgin HDPE Grow Bags (200 to 240 GSM)**:
   - Breathable, puncture-proof, lightweight, and lasts 5+ years under intense rooftop UV.
   - Recommended sizes:
     * **9x9 inch (approx. 5-7 L)**: Leafy greens (spinach, coriander, fenugreek, lettuce, mint).
     * **12x12 inch (approx. 18-20 L)**: Solanaceous crops (tomatoes, capsicum, brinjals, chillies, bush beans).
     * **15x15 to 18x18 inch (approx. 35-50 L)**: Root crops (carrots, radishes, beetroot), cucurbits (gourds, cucumber), and dwarf fruit trees (lemon, pomegranate).
     * **24x24 inch or Rectangular Raised Grow Beds (100+ L)**: Deep rooted perennials and multi-crop guilds (must be positioned over structural beams).
2. **Sub-Irrigated Planters (SIP / EarthBox Systems)**:
   - Feature a bottom water reservoir with an overflow drain and a soil wicking chamber.
   - Reduces evaporative water loss by up to 50% on exposed breezy rooftops, preventing water stress and nutrient leaching.

## 2. Rooftop Microclimate Controls

Rooftops encounter extreme environmental pressures: air temperatures 5–8°C higher due to concrete thermal radiation, low relative humidity, and high wind velocities (30–60% higher than ground level).

### 1. Agro-Shade Netting:
- **Specification**: **50% UV-Stabilized HDPE Shade Net (Green or Green/Black combination)**.
- **Benefits**:
  * Diffuses intense UV-B radiation, preventing leaf chlorosis and blossom drop.
  * Reduces ambient leaf canopy temperature by 4°C to 6°C during peak noon hours.
  * Preserves soil moisture in container mixes.
- **Structural Installation**: Must be erected on a rigid modular GI pipe frame (minimum height 7.5 to 8 feet) with slope to allow wind shedding and rainwater runoff.

### 2. Windbreak Management:
- Unchecked high-velocity rooftop winds cause excessive transpirational water loss, stem snapping, and flower abscission.
- **Living Windbreaks**: Place hardy, wind-resistant perennial plants (Lemongrass, Vetiver, Curry Leaf, Dwarf Moringa in heavy tubs) along the prevailing windward terrace perimeter.
- **Porous Windbreak Screens**: Install 30–50% permeable mesh screens or bamboo trellis lattices. Porous screens gently decelerate air velocity without creating violent turbulent eddy currents behind solid walls.

## 3. Companion Planting Guilds for Urban Rooftops

Companion planting mimics natural symbiotic polycultures, repelling pests, attracting pollinators, and maximizing yield per square meter.

### Proven Companion Pairings:
1. **The Classic Solanaceous Guild (Tomato + Basil + Marigold)**:
   - **Tomatoes**: Heavy fruit producer requiring warm conditions.
   - **Sweet Basil (*Ocimum basilicum*)**: Aromatic terpenes deter hornworms, thrips, and aphids while enhancing tomato sugar profiles.
   - **French Marigold (*Tagetes patula*)**: Roots exude *alpha-terthienyl*, an organic nematicide that eliminates harmful root-knot nematodes (*Meloidogyne*), while bright yellow flowers attract hoverflies and ladybugs.
2. **The Urban Three Sisters Guild (Corn + Climbing Beans + Zucchini/Squash)**:
   - **Sweet Corn**: Acts as a natural vertical climbing trellis.
   - **Climbing Beans**: Fixes atmospheric nitrogen via *Rhizobium* in the potting substrate.
   - **Zucchini / Bush Squash**: Broad, prickly leaves act as a living mulch, shading the potting mix to suppress evaporation and deter crawling pests.
3. **Pollinator Attractor Guild**:
   - **Borage (*Borago officinalis*) + Strawberries**: Borage blooms continuously, producing rich blue nectar that attracts honeybees and bumblebees, boosting strawberry fruit pollination and size.
4. **Pest-Repelling Allium Intercropping**:
   - Plant Garlic, Onions, and Chives around the perimeter of Brassica (cabbage, cauliflower, kale) and Carrot containers to mask plant scent and confuse cabbage white butterflies and carrot rust flies.
