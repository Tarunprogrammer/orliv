## 1. Biological Control Agents (Antagonists & Bio-Pesticides)

Biological control utilizes natural predators, parasitoids, and entomopathogenic microorganisms to keep pest populations below economic threshold levels without synthetic toxins.

### Key Microbial Bio-Pesticides:
1. **Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt)**:
   - Gram-positive soil bacterium producing endotoxin cry-proteins.
   - Specific strains target leaf-eating caterpillars (*Lepidoptera*), beetles (*Coleoptera*), and fly larvae (*Diptera*) without harming beneficial honeybees or ladybird beetles.
2. **NPV (Nuclear Polyhedrosis Virus)**:
   - Highly targeted viral bio-agent for controlling persistent caterpillar species (such as *Helicoverpa armigera* / American bollworm and *Spodoptera litura* / tobacco caterpillar).
3. **Beauveria bassiana (Entomopathogenic Fungus)**:
   - Specific strains (e.g., **strain Bb 147**) penetrate insect cuticles with fungal hyphae, controlling corn borers, thrips, and whiteflies.
4. **Trichoderma species (Antagonistic Bio-Fungus)**:
   - *Trichoderma harzianum* and *Trichoderma viride* actively parasitize pathogenic fungi in soil, preventing damping-off, root rot, and collar rot in vegetable nurseries.
5. **Trichogramma wasps**:
   - Tiny egg parasitoids widely released to parasitize and destroy lepidopteran pest eggs (e.g., bollworms, borers).

## 2. Proven Botanical Insecticide & Repellent Formulations

### 1. Neem Tree (*Azadirachta indica*) Preparations:
- **Azadirachtin**: Potent insect antifeedant, repellent, and growth disruptor (IGR) affecting caterpillars, thrips, and whiteflies.
- **Neem Cake Application**:
  * Incorporate **200 grams of neem cake per m²** directly into the planting pit or potting substrate.
  * Repels and kills root-knot nematodes (*Meloidogyne*), while translocating systemic azadirachtin into plant roots.

### 2. Pyrethrum (*Chrysanthemum cinerariifolium*):
- Flower heads contain natural **pyrethrins**, which attack the nervous system of insects causing immediate knockdown and paralysis.
- Dried flower powder can be dusted directly onto plants or infused in water for contact foliar spraying.

### 3. Hot Chilli Pepper (*Capsicum*) Extract Recipe:
- **Target Pests**: Aphids, ants, small caterpillars, cutworms, and garden snails.
- **Preparation Protocol**:
  1. Grind **200 grams of dry hot chillies** into a fine powder.
  2. Boil in **4 Liters of water** for 15 to 20 minutes.
  3. Strain thoroughly and add another **4 Liters of clean water** (total volume = 8 Liters).
  4. Add a few drops of natural liquid soap (castile or coconut-based) as a wetting/sticking agent.
  5. Spray directly onto infested foliage.

### 4. Garlic (*Allium sativum*) Bio-Repellent:
- Possesses powerful **antifeedant, insecticidal, nematicidal, and repellent** properties due to allicin and sulphur compounds.
- *Caution*: Broad-spectrum garlic sprays can deter beneficial insects if sprayed heavily during daytime flowering; apply late in the evening.

## 3. Physical, Chromatic & Optical Trapping Devices

1. **Yellow Sticky Traps**: Attract and trap winged aphids, whiteflies, fungus gnats, and leafminers.
2. **Blue Sticky Traps**: Highly specific for attracting and monitoring thrips (*Frankliniella occidentalis*).
3. **Pheromone Traps**: Use species-specific female sex pheromones (e.g., *Helilure* for *Helicoverpa*, *Spodolure* for *Spodoptera*) to mass-trap male moths.
4. **Light Traps (Noctuid Traps)**: Use night-time incandescent or UV light bulbs hung above a pan of water with a thin oil film to trap nocturnal moths (armyworms, cutworms).
5. **Homemade Fruit Fly Bottle Traps**: Simple plastic bottles with small side entry holes filled with fermented jaggery water, vinegar, or methyl eugenol to capture *Bactrocera* fruit flies.
